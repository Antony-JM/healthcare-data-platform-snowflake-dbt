# Power BI / Tableau dashboard specification

## 1. Executive Healthcare Operations
KPIs:
- Active patients
- Appointments
- Completed appointments
- No-show rate
- Claims submitted
- Claims denied
- Total billed
- Total paid
- Outstanding balance

## 2. Claims & Revenue
Visuals:
- billed vs paid by month
- denial rate by provider/specialty
- outstanding balance ageing
- claim status trend
- top specialties by billed amount

## 3. Patient Activity
Visuals:
- patients by city
- appointments by specialty
- clinical records by diagnosis
- patient activity trend

## 4. Provider Performance
Visuals:
- appointments by provider
- completed vs cancelled
- claims by provider
- billed and paid amounts

## 5. Data Quality
Visuals:
- source vs target row counts
- duplicate counts
- null counts
- failed dbt tests
- latest successful batch
