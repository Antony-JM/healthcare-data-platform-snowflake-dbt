# Healthcare Business Glossary

- Patient: Individual receiving healthcare services.
- Provider: Clinician delivering or managing healthcare services.
- Insurance: Payer/policy coverage associated with a patient.
- Appointment: Scheduled patient-provider encounter.
- Clinical record: Clinical event or record such as diagnosis/review.
- Claim: Financial request submitted to a payer.
- Billing: Amount raised/adjusted/invoiced for services.
- Payment: Money received against a claim.
- Denied claim: Claim rejected by payer or adjudication process.
- Outstanding balance: Billed amount not yet paid/settled.
